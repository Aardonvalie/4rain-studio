4rain Studio – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

What this app is

4rain Studio is an internal publishing tool used only by the 4rain team. It publishes our own videos (Reels) to Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts that we own and administer, likes posts from those Pages, and reads basic statistics about our own content. It is not offered to the public and no one can sign up to it.

Data we access

Through the Meta Graph API, with the permissions granted by our own administrator account, the app accesses only: the list of our Pages and linked Instagram accounts, the access tokens needed to publish on them, our own posts, and the view, like and comment counts of those posts. If we reply to comments, the app reads the comments left on our own posts in order to answer them.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect, sell or share personal data about Facebook or Instagram users. We do not use the data for advertising, profiling or any purpose other than managing our own content.

Storage and security

Access tokens and publishing logs are stored only on our own computers, are never shared with third parties, and are deleted when they are no longer needed.

Data deletion

The app holds no personal data about other people. To ask for the deletion of any data related to you, or for any question about this policy, contact us at aardon.valie@gmail.com; we will delete it within 30 days.
